*If you only have ten minutes, do this section.*

### Ten-minute physical sweep

- ☐ Run kitchen tap and shower **simultaneously** — pressure holds?
- ☐ Hot water arrival time: ____ sec · stays hot?
- ☐ Flush every toilet
- ☐ Turn on heat, then A/C — both respond?
- ☐ Open, close, and lock every window
- ☐ Test outlets in each room (phone charger)
- ☐ Look under every sink — leaks, stains, warped wood
- ☐ Smell test: mildew · smoke · pets · sewage · air freshener masking something
- ☐ Stand still and listen for 60 seconds. Heard: ______________
- ☐ Cell signal, worst room: ___ bars

### The critical questions

1. What is the **total monthly cost**, rent plus every mandatory fee? $________
2. Which utilities are included, and can I see the last 12 months of actual bills for **this unit**?
3. Deposit, application fee, admin fee — and is the application fee refundable if I'm denied? $________
4. What are the income and credit requirements? ______________
5. How much did rent increase for renewing tenants this year? ______________
6. **Has this unit or building had bed bugs in the last two years?** ______________
7. How much notice do I have to give to not renew, and what happens if I miss it? ____ days
8. What charges are automatic at move-out regardless of condition? ______________
9. Is the building smoke-free, does that include marijuana, and how is it enforced? ______________
10. How do maintenance requests work, typical turnaround, and is there 24/7 emergency? ______________
11. How long has this unit been vacant, and why did the last tenant leave? ______________
12. Is this the actual unit I'd rent, or a model? ☐ Actual ☐ Model

**Closing question:** *"Is there anything about this unit or building you'd want to know if you were me?"*

## 15. Red Flags

- ☐ Agent rushing, dodging, or refusing to show the actual unit
- ☐ Pressure to apply on the spot or pay a "hold fee" today
- ☐ Something the agent said contradicted the listing
- ☐ Common areas noticeably worse than the unit shown
- ☐ Many vacant units / high turnover
- ☐ Fresh paint or caulk in one isolated spot
- ☐ Pest traps, glue boards, or droppings visible
- ☐ Doors or windows that won't latch *(possible settling)*
